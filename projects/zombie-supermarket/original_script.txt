Imagine walking into Walmart to buy a few groceries. You only planned to stay for 15 minutes. But suddenly, people outside start screaming. A man crashes into a parked car. Another person attacks a woman in the middle of the parking lot. Then you notice something terrifying. They're not fighting. They're eating each other. Within minutes, the entire parking lot becomes a nightmare. You run back inside Walmart. The automatic doors close behind you. You think you're safe. But you're wrong. Because over the next 30 days, this supermarket will become your prison. And not everyone inside will survive.
Day 1. Your name is Alex. You're standing in the snack aisle when the emergency alarm suddenly starts ringing. People are running toward the exits. You look through the glass doors and see dozens of infected people attacking anyone they can catch. A security guard tries to lock the entrance. But one infected man grabs his arm. The guard screams. You grab a shopping cart and push it against the doors. Three other survivors help you block the entrance. Inside the store, you find seven more people. A mother with her little daughter. An old man. Two teenagers. A Walmart employee. And a mysterious man wearing a black jacket. Nobody knows what's happening. Your phone has no signal. The news is showing emergency warnings. The government tells everyone to stay indoors. You look outside. Hundreds of infected people are surrounding the building. There's no way out.
Day 3. Three days have passed. The supermarket has become a temporary shelter. You and the other survivors divide the food. Canned beans, bottled water, cereal, and snacks. At first, everyone cooperates. But the food supply is disappearing faster than expected. The mysterious man in the black jacket starts arguing with everyone. He says nobody should be allowed to take more than their share. That night, you hear something moving inside the storage area. You grab a flashlight and investigate. There are empty food boxes scattered across the floor. Someone has been secretly stealing supplies. But that's not the worst discovery. You find a bloody handprint on the storage room door. And then... Something knocks from the other side.
Day 7. A week has passed. The survivors are becoming nervous. The little girl starts crying every night. The old man is getting weaker. And the Walmart employee notices something strange. One of the teenagers has been hiding a bite wound under his sleeve. He's infected. Everyone panics. The mysterious man grabs a metal baseball bat. He wants to throw the teenager outside. But the teenager begs for help. He says he was bitten while trying to save his brother. You have to make a decision. Do you protect him? Or do you risk everyone's life? Before anyone can answer... The teenager suddenly attacks the old man. The room explodes into chaos. You pull the little girl away. The security doors are closed. But now the danger is inside the supermarket.
Day 10. The infected teenager is locked inside a storage room. But the survivors have another problem. The zombies outside have discovered the entrance. Every night, they bang against the glass doors. The barricades are starting to break. You and the Walmart employee move the remaining food to the second floor. You discover a maintenance room with an emergency exit. There might be a way out. But the exit leads directly into the parking lot. And the parking lot is full of zombies. You decide to wait. For now.
Day 15. It's been 15 days. At exactly midnight, every light in Walmart suddenly turns off. The refrigerators stop working. The security cameras go dark. And the emergency lights begin flashing red. You hear footsteps coming from the aisles. Someone is walking around. You turn on your flashlight. Nothing. Then you see a shadow moving between the shelves. It's not one of the survivors. It's a zombie that somehow entered through the loading dock. You run. The others start screaming. The zombie attacks the mysterious man. He fights back, but gets knocked down. You manage to lock the loading dock door. But when you turn around... The mysterious man is gone. Nobody knows if he escaped. Or if he became one of them.
Day 21. Three weeks. Food is almost gone. Water is becoming difficult to find. The mother and her daughter are getting weaker. You decide it's time to use the emergency exit. But when you enter the maintenance room, you discover something shocking. The mysterious man is alive. He's been hiding in the maintenance tunnels. And he has a radio. He tells you that a military evacuation point is operating outside the city. Rescue helicopters are expected in two days. But there's a problem. He refuses to let everyone come. He says there's only enough room for three people. You argue. The mother begs him to help her daughter. Then he pulls out a weapon. He wants to leave alone. Suddenly, a loud crash shakes the building. The zombies have broken through the main entrance. There is no more time.
Day 30. Thirty days. You have survived an entire month inside Walmart. The survivors gather near the emergency exit. You have one chance. You open the maintenance door. Outside, the parking lot is covered with abandoned cars. Zombies are everywhere. You hold the little girl's hand. The Walmart employee carries the remaining water. The mother follows behind. You run between the cars. One zombie notices you. Then another. Within seconds, dozens begin chasing the group. You reach an abandoned delivery truck. The employee manages to start it. Everyone climbs inside. The truck speeds away. For the first time in 30 days... You feel free. But then the radio turns on. A voice says: "Attention. All evacuation centers have been compromised. Do not approach the military base." You look through the windshield. In the distance, you see smoke rising above the city. The mother starts crying. The little girl asks: "Are we safe now?" You don't answer. Because you suddenly notice something. The Walmart employee is sitting quietly in the back. His hand is covered with blood. And on his wrist... There is a fresh bite mark.
You survived 30 days inside Walmart. You escaped the zombies. You saved the little girl. But now someone in your group is infected. Would you leave him behind?
